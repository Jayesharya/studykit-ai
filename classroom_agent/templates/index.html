Classroom Signal / Local-first classroom supportAttendance is an aid to verify, not an automated decision.
PRIVACY / METHOD

Useful context, without surveillance.

Attendance: learners check in with a locally generated QR badge. Check-in duration is recorded; instructors should verify the list.

Room signals: optional camera processing scans QR codes and computes broad frame-to-frame movement. Optional audio processing keeps only short-term volume levels. Raw video and audio are never stored or transmitted.

Limits: movement and sound are not measures of attention, emotion, comprehension, or individual engagement. The local insight agent uses visible rules and offers suggestions only.

Storage: roster, attendance, and coarse session summaries stay in a local SQLite database. No cloud connection is made.
